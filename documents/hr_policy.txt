ACME Corp HR Policy

Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Leave must be approved by the direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.
Unused leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks, fully paid.
Paternity leave is 4 weeks, fully paid.
Adoption leave follows the same rules as maternity leave.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Remote work must be pre-approved by the department head.
All remote employees must be reachable during core hours: 10am to 4pm.

Performance Reviews
Reviews are conducted twice a year: June and December.
Each review includes a self-assessment and a manager assessment.
Salary adjustments are linked to the December review cycle.
